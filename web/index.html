About this simulation

Illustrative surface dynamics on adult anatomical meshes with a fixed skeletal pose. Muscle support changes stiffness and shape; muscles and fat are not separate anatomical layers. The genital mesh has its own response settings and bounded deformation. No tissue self-collision, internal anatomy, or calibrated biological properties. Use this for visual experiments, not medical predictions. Path tracing pauses physics to render the current shape.

Models and skin: MakeHuman / MPFB. External anatomy: MHX2. Asset credits and license details are included. Credits
